Privacy Policy for Bible Verse Memorizer

Last Updated: February 7, 2026

Developer Contact: bibleverseappsupp@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Bible Verse Memorizer ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information directly. However, certain features of the app use third-party services that may collect data as described below.

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Bible verses you add or save
  • Verse references and folders you create
  • App settings and preferences
  • Downloaded verse collections

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

2.2 Permissions Required

The app requests the following permissions:

  • INTERNET: To download Bible verse collections from our website and to use AI search features
  • RECORD_AUDIO: To enable voice search functionality for finding Bible verses
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To send you reminders about memorizing verses
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To allow you to export and import your verse collections
  • VIBRATE: To provide haptic feedback for notifications

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

3.1 Google Gemini AI API

When you use the AI Voice Search feature, the app sends your voice query (converted to text) to Google's Gemini AI service to find relevant Bible verses.

Data collected by Google:

  • Text queries you submit
  • Your IP address
  • Usage metadata

Purpose: To provide AI-powered Bible verse search results

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Google Speech Recognition

When you use the Voice Search feature, Google's Speech Recognition service processes your voice to convert it to text.

Data collected by Google:

  • Audio recordings of your voice
  • Your device's language settings
  • Your IP address

Purpose: To convert your speech to text for searching Bible verses

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Google Play In-App Purchases

If you choose to support the app through donations using the in-app purchase feature, Google Play processes your payment.

Data collected by Google Play:

  • Your Google Play Account ID
  • Purchase history (items purchased, dates, amounts)
  • Email address associated with your Google account

We do not receive or store your payment information. Google handles all payment processing.

Google Play Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 Google AdMob (Advertising)

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data to show you personalized ads.

Data collected by AdMob:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address
  • Usage data and ad interactions

Purpose: To display relevant advertisements

You can remove ads by supporting the app through in-app purchases.

AdMob Privacy Policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties, except as described in Section 3 (Third-Party Services).

The third-party services mentioned above operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

All verse data and app settings are stored locally on your device using encrypted Android Room database. We do not have access to this data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: All verses and settings remain on your device until you manually delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Third-Party Services: Data collected by Google services (Gemini API, Speech Recognition, AdMob, Play Billing) is governed by Google's retention policies.

To delete all local data, simply uninstall the app from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website: https://bobooo76-collab.github.io/bible-verse-box-site/index.html

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access data stored locally on your device
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
