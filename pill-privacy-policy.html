Pill Reminder Plus

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Pill Reminder Plus handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy — your health data stays on your device and is never shared with third parties.

1

Information We Collect

Pill Reminder Plus operates entirely on your device. The app collects and stores the following data locally only:

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules you enter
  • User profiles you create within the app
  • Dose history and adherence logs
  • App preferences (theme, language, notification settings)
We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. Everything remains on your device.
2

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Display your medication schedule and reminders
  • Send local push notifications at the times you set
  • Track your dose history for your personal reference
  • Personalise the app experience (language, theme)
3

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device using the sqflite package. Preferences are stored via shared_preferences. No data leaves your device under any circumstances.

Security of your data depends on your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device secured.

4

Notifications

Pill Reminder Plus uses local notifications to remind you to take your medications. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any network communication or third-party notification services.

You can disable notifications at any time through the app settings or your device's system settings.

5

Third-Party Services

Pill Reminder Plus does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or any social media SDKs
  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Crash reporting services that transmit data externally
6

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your medication and health information is private and remains exclusively on your device.

The export feature (PDF/file export) is entirely user-initiated. Exported files go directly to your chosen destination — we have no access to them.
7

Children's Privacy

Pill Reminder Plus does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app may be used by caregivers to manage medications for children, but all data remains local to the device.

8

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • View all your data directly within the app
  • Delete individual medications or entire profiles
  • Clear all history from the app settings
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all data
9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, feel free to reach out.
